About Me

Hi, I'm Bhaskar.

For 10+ years I've been building sales operations and GTM processes — first at a fast-moving startup in Dublin, then at one of the world's largest professional networks.

At LinkedIn, I've spent the last 5+ years across Sales Strategy and GTM Operations — working on the Enterprise Ads business across EMEA, covering everything from account segmentation and forecasting to executive business reviews. A core part of my work has been driving AI adoption across GTM workflows at scale. Before that, at Ding.com, I spent 3+ years in Pricing and Product Strategy, where I built a rules-based pricing engine and led analytics for digital product strategy and partnerships.

I started AI Sales Advisory because I kept seeing the same thing: small and mid-sized companies want to use AI in their sales process, but they don't have someone internally who's actually done it before. I have. And I want to make that experience accessible to businesses that don't have a LinkedIn-sized budget.
