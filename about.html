About Me

Hi, I'm Bhaskar.

For the last 10+ years I've been building and fixing sales processes — first at a high-growth startup, then at one of the biggest tech companies in the world.

At LinkedIn, I spent 7 years in sales strategy and operations. I was the person who designed several of the internal AI and automation systems that the sales teams relied on daily. Before that, at Ding.com, I helped build the pricing and product strategy that supported their push into new global markets.

I started AI Sales Advisory because I kept seeing the same thing: small and mid-sized companies want to use AI in their sales process, but they don't have someone internally who's actually done it before. I have. And I want to make that experience accessible to businesses that don't have a LinkedIn-sized budget.
