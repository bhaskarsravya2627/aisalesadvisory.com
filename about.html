Our background

Our experience is in sales strategy, pricing, sales operations, data and analytics, and building AI tools.

We’ve worked on how sales teams find and win customers, on pricing, and on the reporting that shows where sales are won and lost. And we build AI tools ourselves. So we see both sides: what an agent needs to work reliably, and what it has to deliver for a business where every enquiry counts.

What we believe

Why voice first.

We think voice agents will be one of the main ways small businesses keep pace with bigger competitors. A larger firm can staff the phones late and at weekends; a well-built agent gives a small business the same cover. For many owners, the phone is still where customers go first, which is why voice is what we build first.

Think of the agent as cover for the gaps in your day, not a replacement for you. It picks up when you can’t (after hours, on holiday, in the middle of a job) and passes anything that needs you straight to you. You stay in control.

But a tool only helps if it fits how you sell. That’s why we start with your sales and operations, not the technology.
