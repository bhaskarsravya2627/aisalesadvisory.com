Privacy notice

How we use
your information.

What we collect when you visit this site or book a call, why, who helps us handle it and the rights you have. Last updated 9 November 2026.

What we collectCookiesYour rights
01 · Who we are

The short version.

AI Sales Advisory runs this website and decides how the personal data described here is used, which makes us the controller of that data. We are a small business and have not appointed a Data Protection Officer.

For anything in this notice, including using your rights, email privacy@aisalesadvisory.com. We don’t sell personal data, and we don’t use it to make automated decisions about you.

02 · What we collect

What we collect and why.

When you book a free AI sales review

Through the Cal.com calendar on our booking page we receive your name, email address, the time you choose and anything you write in the booking notes. We use it to arrange, confirm and hold the call and to prepare for it. Lawful basis: steps you ask us to take before any contract, or our legitimate interest in replying to people who ask to speak to us.

During and after the call

We keep notes about your business, the problems you describe and the next steps we suggest, so we can follow up as agreed and prepare a proposal if you ask for one. Same lawful basis as above.

When you browse the site

Your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, which our host uses to serve the site securely. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website.

Analytics, only if you accept

If you click “Accept analytics”, Google Analytics records how you use the site: pages visited, how you arrived, device type, approximate location and which buttons you click. We use it to improve the site. Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Please don’t put sensitive information, or your customers’ personal data, in booking notes. If a project later needs real customer data, we agree how it is handled in writing first.

03 · Who helps us

Who we share it with.

Netlify

Hosts this website and processes technical data such as IP addresses to serve pages and protect against abuse. Data may be processed in the United States and on Netlify’s global network.

Cal.com

Runs our booking calendar and sends confirmations and reminders. The calendar loads from Cal.com when you open the booking page, so Cal.com receives your IP address even if you don’t book. Data is processed in the United States.

Microsoft 365

Runs our business email, our calendar and Microsoft Teams. Booking details are copied into our calendar and shared mailbox, and the review takes place on Microsoft Teams. Data may be processed in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and the United States.

Google Analytics

Only if you accept analytics cookies. Google processes usage data for us and may process it in the United States. Advertising features are switched off.

Where data leaves the UK we rely on the safeguards UK law allows, such as the UK–US data bridge or the international data transfer terms in each provider’s data processing agreement. We may also share information with professional advisers or authorities where the law requires it.

04 · Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies.

Your cookie choice

We store whether you accepted or rejected analytics in your browser’s local storage, so we don’t ask again on every page. This is strictly necessary.

Google Analytics cookies

_ga and _ga_*, set only after you click “Accept analytics”. They last up to two years and let Google Analytics tell visits apart.

Booking calendar

On the booking page, Cal.com and its security provider may set a short-lived security cookie to tell people from bots. This is strictly necessary for the calendar to work.

Changing your mind

Use “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page to accept or reject analytics. Rejecting removes our analytics cookies from your browser.

If we add another analytics tool, the cookie banner will name it and we will update this notice first.

05 · How long we keep it

How long we keep it.

  1. Booking details and call notes for a review that doesn’t lead to work: up to 12 months after the call, then deleted.
  2. Client records such as contracts and invoices: six years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as UK tax rules require.
  3. Analytics data: no longer than 14 months, as set in Google Analytics.
  4. Website logs: as set by our host for security and troubleshooting.
06 · Your rights

Your rights.

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it (including an absolute right to object to direct marketing), take it with you where the law allows, and withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it. We reply within one month, and it’s normally free.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, please tell us first and we’ll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

07 · Voice agents we build

If you’re a client.

When we build or support a voice agent for your business, your business is the controller of the data your callers provide, such as recordings, transcripts and booking details, and we act as your processor. We agree a data processing agreement with you that covers what we process, the providers we use, where data is stored, how long it is kept and how it is deleted.

We update this notice when the way we use personal data changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
