Good questions

Before you put AI on the phone.

The practical answers founders and operators need before investing.

Will it sound robotic?+

It should not. We tune voice, pacing, interruption handling and dialogue for the context. More importantly, the agent stays concise and useful rather than trying to impersonate a person.

Can it connect to our current systems?+

Yes. We scope integrations around the outcome—typically telephony, calendars, CRMs, messaging, knowledge sources and custom APIs. We confirm feasibility before build.

What happens when the AI is unsure?+

Every solution includes boundaries and escalation paths. Depending on the situation, it can ask for clarification, transfer, arrange a callback or capture the request for a human.

Can agencies or software firms resell it?+

Yes. Our white-label model supports your branding and commercial offer while we help provide the product and technical capability behind it.
