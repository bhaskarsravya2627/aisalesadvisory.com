Good questions

Before you put AI on the phone.

The commercial, operational and safety questions worth answering first.

How will we know it is worth the investment?+

We compare results with what happens today. Depending on your goal, that could mean more appointments booked, more enquiries followed up, faster responses or less time spent handling routine calls. We agree the measures before a first rollout.

Can it connect to our current systems?+

We review the systems you use and agree which connections are needed. These may include your phone service, calendar, customer database, booking platform or other business software.

What happens when the AI is unsure or gets something wrong?+

We agree what the solution can handle and when your team should step in. It can ask for more detail, transfer the call, arrange a callback or record the request for a person to review.

How are privacy, consent and call recording handled?+

Requirements vary by workflow and technology stack. We identify the applicable data, disclosure, access, retention and recording requirements during design rather than treating them as an afterthought.

What will the strategy call cover?+

We discuss the customer conversation, what you want to improve, the systems involved and who owns the workflow. You leave with a clearer view of fit and a useful next step.

How are scope, price and timing agreed?+

They depend on what the solution needs to do, the systems it connects to, call volumes and the support you need. We discuss these with you and agree the work, price and timing before it starts.

What should not be automated?+

Conversations requiring sensitive judgement, unsupported advice or unclear accountability should remain with people. We design automation around defined tasks and clear transfer conditions.
