Good questions

Before you put AI on the phone.

The commercial, operational and safety questions worth answering first.

How do we know the economics work?+

We begin with a baseline tied to the workflow: jobs captured, quotes progressed, payments resolved, handling time or service capacity. A pilot is assessed against agreed measures before wider investment.

Can it connect to our current systems?+

We confirm feasibility during scoping. Depending on the job, that may include telephony, job management, CRM, calendars, accounting, messaging, knowledge sources or a relevant API.

What happens when the AI is unsure or gets something wrong?+

Each workflow has explicit boundaries, confirmations, test scenarios and escalation paths. The agent can clarify, transfer, arrange a callback or stop and capture the request for human review.

How are privacy, consent and call recording handled?+

Requirements vary by workflow and technology stack. We identify the applicable data, disclosure, access, retention and recording requirements during design rather than treating them as an afterthought.

What should not be automated?+

Conversations requiring sensitive judgement, unsupported advice or unclear accountability should remain with people. We design automation around defined tasks and clear transfer conditions.
