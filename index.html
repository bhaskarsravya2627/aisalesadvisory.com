Good questions

Before you start with AI.

The questions owners usually ask first.

Is this only about voice agents?+

No. Voice agents are our main focus, because the phone is where many enquiries go unanswered. But we start with how you sell and run the business, and recommend what fits. That might be follow-up messages, better use of tools you already have, or no AI at all.

We’re a small business. Is this for us?+

Yes. We work with owner-led small and medium-sized businesses. If you’re the one answering the phone, chasing quotes and doing the admin, you’re exactly who we have in mind.

What happens in the free AI sales review?+

It’s a 30-minute call. We talk through how customers reach you, where enquiries or follow-ups slip and which tools you use. You come away with an honest view of where you’re losing sales and a suggested first step. The review is free. An AI growth plan, if you want one, is a separate piece of work that we scope and agree with you first.

Do I keep my phone number?+

Yes, in most cases. Your number stays the same, and calls can be forwarded to the agent when you don’t pick up, outside opening hours, or all the time if you prefer. We set this up with your phone provider. Urgent or safety-critical calls, such as someone reporting a gas smell, go straight to a person under the rules you set.

Will my customers know they’re speaking to AI?+

We recommend being upfront about it, and we design how and when the agent says so with you. We also make it easy for customers to reach a person whenever they need one.

What happens when the agent can’t help or gets it wrong?+

We agree in advance what it handles and what it doesn’t. Anything that needs judgement, specialist advice or a sensitive conversation stays with people. When the agent is unsure, it can ask for more detail, transfer the call, arrange a callback or note the request for you to review.

Do I need to be technical?+

No. We set up the connections, explain what the agent does and show you how to check its calls. You set the rules; we handle the build and look after it once it’s live.

Will it work with the tools we already use?+

We check before we build. We look at your phone line, calendar, booking system and any software you keep customer records in, and agree which connections the agent needs.

How will I know it’s worth it?+

We compare results with what happens today. Depending on your goal, that could mean more enquiries answered, more quotes followed up, more appointments booked or less time spent on routine calls. We agree the measures before a pilot starts.

How are privacy, consent and call recording handled?+

Requirements vary by business and by the tools involved. We work through data, disclosure, access, retention and recording during design, not as an afterthought.

How are scope, price and timing agreed?+

They depend on what you need, the tools involved and the support you want. We agree the work, price and timing with you before anything starts.

Do you work with agencies?+

Yes. We design and build voice agents that agencies and software firms offer to their own clients under their brand. How partnerships work.
