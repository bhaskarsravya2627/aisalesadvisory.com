02 · Service business receptionist

Make every customer call a useful next step.

For plumbing, heating, electrical and other service teams balancing customer calls with the work already on their schedule.

SERVICE RECEPTIONISTILLUSTRATIVE CONVERSATION
“I need someone to look at a leaking tap. Do you have an appointment?”
“I can help with that. Let me take a few details and check the available times.”
✓
THE NEXT STEPA qualified enquiry and a clear hand-off
WHAT A FULL SOLUTION COULD DO
  • Capture the job. Understand what the customer needs, where they are and how to reach them.
  • Book with confidence. Check available appointments, confirm the details and create a booking in the agreed calendar.
  • Route the exceptions. Recognise requests that need urgent attention or human judgement and follow the agreed escalation path.
  • Keep the team informed. Record the outcome and pass useful context to the right person or system.

Availability, booking and escalation depend on the business’s rules, staffing and connected systems.
