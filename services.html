02 · Build

Custom voice agents

Every agent we build is designed around your customers, your rules, your systems and your brand. It answers calls, takes enquiries, books appointments and follows up quotes, and hands the call to a person whenever it should.

Built around your problem

We start from where you’re losing sales, then design the conversation, rules and handovers to fit how you work.

What happens after the call

Follow-up messages, record updates, tasks and handovers are set up too, so the next step actually happens.

Example we’ve built: travel

Problem: travellers call when the office is closed. Our Travel Sales & Care prototype helps them explore options, supports existing bookings and hands to a person when needed.

Example we’ve built: service

Problem: calls missed while you’re on a job. Our Service Desk prototype captures the job, checks how urgent it is and books the visit.

02 · Build · Focused pilot

Every build starts with a pilot

This is the detail behind step three of how we work, Build & pilot. We take one type of call, agree what success looks like, and run the agent on real enquiries before deciding together whether to expand.

Choose one job

One type of call, such as after-hours enquiries or quote follow-up, with a clear goal.

Agree the measures

What you’ll judge it on, such as bookings made or quotes followed up, and how that compares with today.

Test before it goes live

We try it on realistic calls, including the awkward ones, and set the points where a person takes over.

Review and decide

We look at the results together. You decide whether to expand it, adjust it or stop.

03 · Support

After it goes live

A voice agent isn’t finished on launch day. Your prices change, customers ask new questions and your rules move on, so we keep looking after it with you and you’re never left alone with the technology.

Review calls and handovers

We look at what callers ask, how the agent answers and where a person on your team had to step in.

Tune and improve

We refine the conversation, rules and handovers so the agent gets better at helping your customers.

Keep it up to date

When your prices, services, hours or offers change, we update the agent so customers get the right answer.

Report on results

We tell you what the agent is doing for your sales and agree what to improve next.
