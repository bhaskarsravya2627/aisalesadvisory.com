Services

Here's How I Can Help

Every business is different, so every engagement is tailored. But here's the range of things I typically work on with clients.

Service 01

AI Sales Strategy & Roadmapping

This is for teams that know AI should be part of their sales process but don't have a clear plan for how. I'll look at how your team sells today — the workflows, the tools, the data — and build a realistic roadmap for where AI fits in.

You won't get a 100-slide deck full of buzzwords. You'll get a practical plan you can actually execute.

  • Audit of your current sales workflows and tech stack
  • Assessment of where your team and data stand on AI readiness
  • Prioritised list of opportunities ranked by expected impact
  • Phased plan with specific tool recommendations
  • A presentation you can take to your leadership team or board
Let's Talk Strategy
Service 02

Go-to-Market AI Acceleration

You already have a sales motion running. Good. This engagement is about making it significantly better with AI — not ripping it up, but upgrading the parts that matter most.

I focus on the practical stuff: which leads to call first, how to personalise outreach without it eating your team's day, and how to actually forecast with confidence.

  • Setting up AI-powered lead scoring so your team focuses on the right deals
  • Smarter outreach — personalisation that scales without sounding robotic
  • Pipeline monitoring that catches problems before they become missed quarters
  • Competitive intelligence you can act on, not just read
  • Making your existing CRM and tools work harder for you
Accelerate Your GTM
Service 03

AI Literacy & Leadership Coaching

If your sales leaders don't understand AI, adoption won't happen. It's that simple. These sessions are designed for senior people who need to make decisions about AI tooling but don't have the technical background to evaluate what's real and what's marketing.

I make it practical: real demos, real tools, real scenarios from your industry. No jargon marathons.

  • Half-day or full-day workshops for your leadership team
  • Live demos with actual AI sales tools (not slide decks about them)
  • A framework for evaluating AI vendors so you don't get sold nonsense
  • Guidance on how to roll out AI adoption without your team resisting it
  • Ongoing 1:1 coaching if you want continued support
Book a Workshop
Service 04

Sales Process Automation

This is for teams who know their reps are spending too much time on manual, repetitive work. I map out your entire sales process, find the biggest time-wasters, and design automations that your team can implement without needing a full engineering squad.

  • End-to-end process mapping of your sales cycle
  • Scoring each automation opportunity by effort vs. payoff
  • Recommendations on tools — what to use and what to skip
  • Implementation blueprints your team (or a freelancer) can build from
  • A way to measure whether the automation is actually saving time
Let's Map Your Process
